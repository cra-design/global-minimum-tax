Who must file returns

The Global Minimum Tax Act applies to qualifying MNE groups for fiscal years that begin on or after December 31, 2023.

A [at least one?] member of a qualifying multinational enterprise (MNE) groups must file with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) for a particular fiscal year, when the group has a constituent entity located in Canada for the fiscal year.

The member must file one or more of the following returns:

  • GloBE Information Return (GIR)
  • Global Minimum Tax Return
  • GIR Notification

A constituent entity or person may be appointed to file on behalf of all constituent entities or persons that must file with the CRA.

Determine which returns to file

To determine which entities must file a GIR, a Global Minimum Tax Return or a GIR notification with the CRA, use the following interactive questions.

Who must file a Global Minimum Tax Return

Each person who is liable to pay tax under Part 2 or Part 3 of the Act, or each Canadian filing entity, must file a Global Minimum Tax Return with the CRA.

Who must file a GIR notification

If a qualifying foreign filing entity is filing the GIR for the MNE group with the tax authority of the jurisdiction where it is located, each constituent entity located in Canada, or a designated notification entity must file a GIR notification with the CRA.

Who can file each return on your behalf [to be incorporated in the answers]

Who can file a Global Minimum Tax Return on your behalf

If you are filing a Global Minimum Tax Return and more than one person in respect of your MNE group is required to file a GMTR for a fiscal year, one of those persons who is resident in Canada may be appointed as a Canadian filing entity.

Who can file a GIR notification on your behalf

If you are filing a GIR Notification and more than one constituent entity of a qualifying MNE group is required to notify the CRA for a fiscal year of a qualifying foreign filing entity, one of those constituent entities that are located in Canada may be appointed as a designated notification entity.

Qualified Income Inclusion Rule

A qualified Income Inclusion Rule (IIR) for a fiscal year means an IIR that has qualified status (including transitional qualified status) for the fiscal year as determined by the Inclusive Framework and published on the website of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development.

Qualified Undertaxed Profit Rule

A qualified Undertaxed Profit Rule (UTPR), for a fiscal year, means a UTPR that has qualified status (including transitional qualified status) for the fiscal year as determined by the Inclusive Framework and published on the website of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development.

Designated filing entity

A designated filing entity refers to a constituent entity within a multinational enterprise (MNE) group, excluding the ultimate parent entity, that has been appointed by the group to submit the GloBE Information Return (GIR) on its behalf. This entity must be located in a jurisdiction that has both of the following:

  • a qualifying Competent Authority Agreement in place with the implementing jurisdictions where the GIR will be exchanged
  • a qualified Income Inclusion Rule (IIR) or a qualified Undertaxed Profit Rule (UTPR)

For information about Competent Authority Agreements, refer to: Competent Authority Agreements and Notices.

Designated local entity

A designated local entity refers to a constituent entity within an multinational enterprise (MNE) group located in Canada and designated by other Canadian constituent entities of the group to file the GloBE Information Return (GIR). However, the designation of a local entity does not guarantee that the GIR will be exchanged with other implementing jurisdictions.

Qualifying foreign filing entity

A qualifying foreign filing entity (QFFE) is an ultimate parent entity or a designated filing entity of the MNE group that is located in a foreign jurisdiction and that is obligated to file a GloBE information return with the tax authority of the foreign jurisdiction, provided that the foreign jurisdiction has a qualifying competent authority agreement that is in effect on or before the GloBE information return exchange date for the fiscal year. Canada must be a party to that agreement for the entity to be a QFFE.

Constituent entity

A constituent entity refers to any entity or permanent establishment that is part of the multinational enterprise (MNE) group subject to the global minimum tax rules.

Low-tax entity

A low-tax entity means a constituent entity of an MNE group that is located in either of:

  • (a) a low-tax jurisdiction
  • (b) a jurisdiction that would be a low-tax jurisdiction, if the effective tax rate of the MNE group for the jurisdiction were determined without regard to any income or expense recorded by an entity in respect of an intragroup financing arrangement.
